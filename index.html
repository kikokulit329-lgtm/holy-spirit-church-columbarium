A sacred space for remembrance

The Columbarium

A peaceful place to remember.

Our columbarium offers families a dignified and prayerful setting for the cremated remains of loved ones. Designed as an extension of our spiritual community, it provides a place to visit, reflect, pray, and keep cherished memories close.

  • Dedicated memorial niches
  • Peaceful prayer and reflection spaces
  • Family-oriented arrangements and guidance
  • A sacred setting connected to the church community
